What Tax Incentives Are Available to an Exporting Business in Türkiye?

Serdar Saraç, Certified Public Accountant · Last updated: 18.11.2026

In brief

  • No VAT is charged on exports; VAT borne on export-related costs that cannot be offset can be refunded. Purchases from a manufacturer for export, or under an inward processing authorisation, can be made without paying VAT up front.
  • For certain services provided abroad, 100% of the income is deducted from the tax base; companies exporting goods pay 20% corporate tax on export income in 2026. From 2027, the rate on manufacturing income of registered industrial producers will be 12.5%.
  • Each incentive depends on documents and deadlines: export proceeds must be brought into Türkiye within 180 days and at least 35% sold to a bank; service-export income must be brought in by the tax-return deadline.
What are my tax advantages in export transactions?

This question came in through the reader-question form under the topic of foreign trade, and has been made general. The short answer: exports do not come with a single tax advantage. VAT, income or corporate tax, stamp duty and fees, and foreign-exchange rules have to be looked at together. Which of them apply, and to what extent, depends on three questions:

  • Is the business exporting goods or services?
  • Does it manufacture the goods itself, or buy and export them?
  • Is it a sole proprietorship or a company?

The sections below take each topic in turn with these three questions in mind.

1. VAT: exemption and refund

Export of goods. No VAT is charged on goods delivered to a buyer abroad that leave the customs territory (VAT Law No. 3065, Art. 11/1-a and 12/1). This is a full exemption: VAT paid in Türkiye on costs related to the exported goods is deductible. The part that cannot be offset may be refunded in cash or set off against other tax debts (Art. 32). The documents required and the route to the refund depend on the amount and on whether a sworn-in CPA report, a guarantee or a tax audit is used. The refund file should therefore be built while the transaction takes place.

Export of services. No VAT is charged when a service is provided for a customer abroad and the benefit of the service is used abroad (Art. 11/1-a and 12/2). The invoice must be issued to the customer abroad and payment received from that customer. If the benefit of the service arises in Türkiye, the exemption does not apply even if the invoice is addressed abroad.

Deliveries for export. When a manufacturer delivers goods to an exporter on condition that they are exported, the manufacturer shows VAT on the invoice but does not collect it (Art. 11/1-c). The exporter must export the goods within three months from the beginning of the month following delivery. If the goods are not exported in time, the uncollected tax is claimed from the exporter. The manufacturer may claim a refund of the VAT it bore on such deliveries.

Inward processing regime. Inputs for products to be exported can be sourced under this regime.

  • Imported inputs: customs duties and import VAT are secured by a guarantee until the export takes place.
  • Domestic inputs: can be purchased without paying VAT under an inward processing authorisation certificate (DİİB). The legal basis is provisional Article 17 of the VAT Law, extended to 31.12.2030 by Law No. 7573 (Official Gazette 29.01.2026).

The exporter therefore does not have to pay VAT on inputs and then wait for a refund. However, if the export committed to in the certificate does not take place in time, the taxes are claimed together with late-payment interest.

2. Income and corporate tax

100% income deduction for service exports. From 2026 income onwards, all of the income from providing the services listed in the law to customers abroad is deducted from the tax base (Income Tax Law Art. 89/13, Corporate Tax Law Art. 10/1-ğ; Presidential Decision No. 11257, Official Gazette 30.04.2026). The services are:

  • architecture, engineering, design and software;
  • medical reporting and bookkeeping;
  • call centres;
  • product testing and certification;
  • data storage, data processing and data analysis;
  • education and health services under the permission and supervision of the relevant ministry.

The condition is that the payment is brought into Türkiye by the tax-return deadline. The list is exhaustive; services that are not on it, such as general consultancy, are outside its scope.

Reduced rate for companies exporting goods. The corporate tax rate is reduced by 5 percentage points on income derived exclusively from exports by exporting companies (Corporate Tax Law Art. 32/7). For 2026 the general rate is 25% and the rate on export income is 20%. This reduction applies only to corporate tax; there is no similar reduction in the income tax schedule for sole proprietorships.

A new rate for manufacturers from 2027. Article 32/8 of the Corporate Tax Law was rewritten by Law No. 7582 (Official Gazette 04.06.2026):

  • A 12.5% rate will apply to income derived exclusively from manufacturing by companies that hold an industrial registry certificate and actually manufacture. This applies to income for 2027 and later years.
  • No separate export reduction applies to manufacturing income within this scope, so income from goods manufactured and exported is also taxed at 12.5%.
  • The 5-point export reduction continues for income from goods bought and then exported.
  • For 2026 income, the previous 1-point reduction for manufacturing continues to apply.

Mind the minimum corporate tax. From 2025, corporate tax cannot be lower than 10% of income before deductions and exemptions (Corporate Tax Law Art. 32/C). The real effect of the deductions and rates above must therefore be calculated separately for each company and compared with the minimum tax.

3. Stamp duty and fees

Certain documents issued for exports, sales and deliveries treated as exports, and foreign-currency-earning services and activities may be exempt from stamp duty. Certain transactions for the same activities may also be exempt from fees. The legal basis is Additional Article 2 of Stamp Duty Law No. 488 and Additional Article 1 of Fees Law No. 492. The exemption does not apply automatically; the link between the transaction and the export must be documented, for example by:

  • an inward processing authorisation certificate;
  • other documents required by the relevant legislation.

The scope of the exemption should therefore be checked contract by contract and transaction by transaction.

4. Foreign exchange: obligations and support

This is not a tax topic as such, but it directly affects the exporter's cash flow and the conditions of the incentives above.

  • Bringing in export proceeds: export proceeds must be brought into Türkiye within 180 days of the actual export date (Communiqué 2018-32/48, Art. 3).
  • Mandatory sale: at least 35% of export proceeds must be sold to the bank that issues the export proceeds acceptance certificate (İBKB) or the foreign-exchange purchase certificate (DAB). This temporary rate has been extended until 31 January 2027.
  • Currency conversion support: the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye supports the conversion of foreign currency earned abroad into Turkish lira. The basic rate is 2%; the temporary 3% rate has also been extended until 31 January 2027. From 1 October 2026:
    • the support is tied to a sales limit calculated from the company's value added, profitability and labour costs;
    • companies whose foreign-currency position ratio exceeds 10% are not eligible;
    • the annual cap is TRY 100 million;
    • in the last quarter of 2026 the limits apply at one quarter.

Where to start in practice

  • Clarify the nature of the activity. Goods or services; manufacturing or trading? Which incentive applies depends on this distinction.
  • Separate export income in the accounts. The reduced rates apply to income derived "exclusively" from exports or manufacturing. If this income is not tracked separately, calculating and proving the reduction becomes difficult.
  • Put the deadlines in the calendar: three months for deliveries for export, 180 days for bringing in proceeds, the tax-return deadline for the service-export deduction, and the term of the inward processing certificate.
  • Build the refund file from the start. Keeping customs declarations, invoices, foreign-exchange purchase certificates and the list of VAT borne in order at the time of each transaction shortens the refund process.
