Services

Scope of services

The work carried out by the office within the framework of Law No. 3568 on Certified Public Accountants and Sworn-in Certified Public Accountants is listed below.

01

Accounting and bookkeeping

  • Bookkeeping on a balance-sheet or business-account basis
  • e-Ledger, e-Invoice, e-Archive and e-Waybill procedures
  • Year-end procedures and financial statements
  • Fixed-asset and depreciation records

02

Tax returns

  • VAT, withholding tax and social security premium returns
  • Provisional tax, corporate tax and income tax returns
  • Stamp duty and other filing obligations
  • Tax calendar tracking and assessment checks

03

Payroll and social security

  • Monthly payroll and salary calculations
  • Employment start and termination notices, monthly premium declarations
  • Review of employment incentives
  • Severance and notice pay calculations

04

Company procedures

  • Formation of sole proprietorships and companies
  • Capital increases, share transfers and change of company type
  • Branch openings, change of address and liquidation
  • General meetings and trade registry procedures

05

Tax audits and settlement

  • Review of records and documents before an audit
  • Explanations and document preparation during an audit
  • Support in settlement and administrative applications
  • Voluntary disclosure and amended returns

06

Foreign trade

  • VAT refunds and offsetting for exports
  • Inward processing and deliveries for export
  • Import costs, freight and customs records
  • Exchange differences and foreign-currency accounting

07

Foreign-owned companies

  • Company formation in Türkiye and foreign-shareholder procedures
  • Foreign direct investment notifications
  • Periodic reporting to shareholders in English
  • Transfer pricing documentation

08

Financial reporting and budgeting

  • Monthly management reports
  • Budget preparation and budget-to-actual comparison
  • Cash-flow and cost analyses
  • Project-based profitability reports

Related training: Tax Consequences of Inflation Accounting (Deloitte) · Budget Planning and Control (Istanbul University)

09

Audit and internal control

  • External audit of cooperatives
  • Review of internal control systems
  • Assessment of process and authority matrices
  • Audit preparation

Related certificate and training: Cooperative External Auditor (TÜRMOB) · Internal Control and Internal Audit in Companies (PwC)

Professional fees are agreed in a written engagement letter, based on the minimum fee schedule published each year by TÜRMOB, the Union of Chambers of Certified Public Accountants of Türkiye.
