2. Personal data processed

No personal data is collected through this website. The question form of the reader-questions section runs on Microsoft Forms and does not ask for a name, e-mail address or contact details; only the topic and the question text are received. Any personal data contained in a question is removed before publication. If you send a question by e-mail, your e-mail address is processed only to receive the question; it is not published and no individual reply is sent. If you contact us by telephone, e-mail or WhatsApp, the following data may be processed to the extent you provide it:

  • Identity data (name, surname, title)
  • Contact data (telephone number, e-mail address, address)
  • Information about your request and the content of correspondence
  • Where a professional service relationship is established, information and documents that must be kept under Law No. 3568 and tax legislation

3. Purposes of processing

  • Receiving and answering your contact requests
  • Concluding and performing a professional service agreement
  • Fulfilling obligations under Law No. 3568, the Tax Procedure Law and related legislation
  • Establishing, exercising and protecting rights in legal disputes

4. Legal grounds and method of collection

Your personal data is collected by telephone, e-mail, WhatsApp and in face-to-face meetings, and processed on the legal grounds set out in Article 5(2) of the KVKK: that processing is directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract; that it is necessary for the data controller to fulfil a legal obligation; that it is necessary for the establishment, exercise or protection of a right; and that it is necessary for the legitimate interests of the data controller, provided that it does not harm the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject.

5. Transfers

Your personal data may be transferred to authorised public institutions under legal obligations (such as the Revenue Administration, the Social Security Institution, the professional chamber and judicial authorities). Where the servers of IT service providers used for e-mail and file storage are located abroad, transfers are made in accordance with the conditions set out in Article 9 of the KVKK.

6. Retention period

Personal data is kept for as long as the purpose of processing requires and for the retention periods set out in the relevant legislation; at the end of that period it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information if it has been processed; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose; to know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it has been transferred; to request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate; to request deletion or destruction under the conditions set out in the KVKK and to request that these actions be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred; to object to a result against you arising exclusively from analysis by automated systems; and to claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

Cookie policy

This website does not use cookies or similar technologies to track visitors, build profiles or show advertisements. There are no analytics or marketing cookies on the site. To see how many people visit the site, only the cookieless measurement tool listed below is used.

The following services are used to display the pages correctly and to measure total visits. To the extent technically necessary, these services may process connection data such as your IP address:

  • Google Fonts – loading of fonts
  • Google Maps – the location map on the Turkish contact page (loaded only on that page)
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics – measuring total visits and page views. This tool does not use cookies, does not identify visitors and does not track across sites.

Google Maps may use cookies for the operation of its own service; details are available in Google's privacy policy. You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If analytics cookies are used on the site in the future, they will be activated only with your explicit consent and this policy will be updated.
