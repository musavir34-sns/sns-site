Profile

Career

Serdar Saraç graduated in 1999 from the Business Administration department (Accounting and Finance) of Anadolu University.

From 2000 to 2006 he worked as budget and financial control manager at AFM Sinemaları and led the budget and financial control team during the company's initial public offering. In 2007 he served as finance manager at MV Holding, and in 2008–2009 as financial and legal advisor at Altıoklar Film.

From 2009 to 2024 he was director of finance and deputy general manager at TVS Group, responsible for financial reporting, tax and risk across television studio operations, technical equipment, boat building and chartering, and construction.

Since 2024 he has practised as a certified public accountant at his office in Kağıthane, Istanbul, under the name SNS Danışmanlık.
